    NoodleCore Privacy Statement

    Version 1.0 · effective 19 October 2026

    This is the NoodleCore privacy statement. Written to be easy and concrete: what we do (and do not) collect, why, for how long, and what your rights are.

    Who is responsible

    Michael van Erp trading under the name NoodleCore (sole proprietorship, Chamber of Commerce (KvK) number 42185909) is the controller within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

    • Contact: michael@noodlecore.io
    • Mentioning "privacy" ensures your request is handled with priority.
    • See also the contact page.

    The shortest section: what we do not do

    • No cookies. The website places no cookies and asks for no consent, because there is nothing to accept.
    • No analytics. We use no Google Analytics, Matomo or comparable statistics services. We do not know who visits the site; only how often the site is requested in total (raw server logs of the hosting party).
    • No external scripts or trackers. All code and stylesheets are served by our own server. Since 19 October 2026 the fonts are self-hosted too: your browser contacts noodlecore.io only for the entire site — your IP address never goes to Google Fonts or any other third party.
    • No advertising, no profiling, no selling of data. Ever.

    Which data we process, and why

    1. Website visits

    The site is fully static and runs on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). With every request the hosting party registers technical server data (including your IP address, timestamp and requested page). We have no access to and no influence over the content of those logs; GitHub processes them as an independent controller for delivering and securing the service, in accordance with its own privacy policy. We place no tracking technology of any kind ourselves.

    What Why (legal basis)
    Server logs by hosting party Legitimate interest: technically delivering and securing the site (GDPR art. 6.1.f)

    2. Contact by e-mail

    If you mail us, we process your name, e-mail address and the content of your message in order to reply and follow up. E-mail is hosted by Zoho (EU data centres, mail route mx.zoho.eu).

    • Why: performance of a (future) agreement, or our legitimate interest in answering serious questions.
    • Retention period: at most 2 years after the last contact, unless the correspondence is part of our business administration (see 4) or you ask for earlier deletion.

    3. Business contacts and leads

    Company names, names, roles and e-mail addresses of people who contact us or whom we approach for serious business cooperation (quotes, pilots, conferences).

    • Why: legitimate interest — being able to run a company.
    • Retention period: at most 2 years after the last contact; deleted afterwards, unless an agreement follows.

    4. Customer and business administration

    From clients we keep the administration required by law: agreements, quotes, invoices and the accompanying company data.

    • Why: legal obligation — fiscal retention duty (7 years).
    • Retention period: 7 years, in accordance with the fiscal retention duty.

    5. Development process and project data

    Our internal development environment (version control, ticket system, build logs) may contain names and messages of people involved: our own team, contributors and business contacts. These systems run on our own hardware and are not shared with third parties.

    • Why: legitimate interest — building, maintaining and being able to account for our products.
    • Retention period: duration of the project, afterwards archiving of what must remain accountable.

    Recipients (sub-processors and parties)

    We never pass on your data for marketing or advertising. Only these parties necessarily play a role:

    Party Role Why
    GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages) Hosting static website The site has to live somewhere
    Zoho (EU data centres) E-mail hosting E-mail has to arrive somewhere
    Payment service (incl. bunq) Banking and payments Processing payments

    If you use the software yourself in the cloud with a provider, the privacy policy of that platform applies; we have no access there. For work performed for you we conclude a data processing agreement on request.

    Your rights

    Under the GDPR you have the right to:

    1. Access — which data we hold about you;
    2. Rectification — correction of incorrect data;
    3. Erasure — deletion of your data;
    4. Restriction — temporarily processing less;
    5. Data portability — taking your data with you in a common format;
    6. Objection — against processing based on legitimate interest;
    7. Withdrawal of consent — if that was ever the basis.

    Mail michael@noodlecore.io mentioning "privacy". We usually reply within a few business days, and always within the statutory period of one month. You do not need to justify yourself; we may ask who you are, to avoid releasing someone else's data.

    Not satisfied? You can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

    Automated decision-making

    We use no automated decision-making or profiling with personal data.

    Changes

    This statement may change — for example if our services or hosting change. The current version always stands on this page, with version number and date. For far-reaching changes we announce that on the homepage.

    Questions about this document? Mail michael@noodlecore.io — we are happy to explain, in plain language.
